Frequently Asked Questions

Shipping

1. Do you deliver throughout South Africa?

Yes. Auraaya delivers to all major cities and towns across South Africa using reputable courier partners. Standard delivery typically requires 2 to 5 business days from the date of order confirmation.

2. What are the shipping costs?

Complimentary shipping is offered on all orders exceeding R3,500. For orders below R3,500, a flat shipping fee of R120 is applicable. The final shipping cost will be displayed at checkout prior to payment.

3. Do you provide international shipping?

At present, Auraaya ships within South Africa only. International shipping to India and other global destinations is under development. Please follow our official Instagram account @Auraaya_Heritage for future announcements.

4. How can I track my order?

Upon dispatch, you will receive an email containing your tracking number and a direct link to monitor your shipment in real time. For additional assistance, please contact us via the Contact Us page.

5. Is my purchase insured during transit?

Yes. All Auraaya jewellery items are fully insured and packaged in secure, tamper-evident materials to ensure safe delivery.

6. What occurs if I am unavailable to receive delivery?

The courier will leave a collection notice with instructions. You may arrange redelivery or collect the parcel from the designated courier depot within 7 calendar days. A signature is required for all high-value consignments.

7. Can I amend my delivery address after placing an order?

Address changes can be accommodated within 2 hours of order placement. Please email supportaur@auraaya.com immediately with your order number. Once the order has been dispatched, the delivery address cannot be modified.

8. Is express delivery available?

Yes. An express delivery option is available at checkout for delivery within 1 to 2 business days. An additional fee of R250 applies.

9. How is jewellery packaged for shipment?

Each item is presented in a premium Auraaya gift box with anti-tarnish lining, a polishing cloth, and a certificate of authenticity. The packaging is designed to be gift-ready upon arrival.

10. What is the procedure if my order arrives damaged?

Should your order arrive damaged, please contact us within 48 hours of receipt and provide photographic evidence. Auraaya will arrange for a replacement or repair at no additional cost to you.

Returns & Exchanges

1. What is the return period?

Customers may return jewellery items within 14 calendar days of delivery, provided the item is unused, unworn, and in its original condition with all packaging and certificates included.

2. Are all items eligible for return?

Returns are accepted for standard, non-personalised items only. Custom-made, engraved, or altered jewellery is not eligible for return or exchange due to the bespoke nature of these pieces.

3. How do I initiate a return or exchange?

To request a return or exchange, please email auraaya@gmail.com within 14 days of receipt. Include your order number, reason for return, and photographs of the item. Our team will provide a return authorisation and instructions.

4. Who pays for return shipping?

If the item is faulty, damaged, or incorrect, Auraaya will cover return shipping costs. For returns due to change of mind, the customer is responsible for return shipping fees.

5. Will I receive a full refund?

Approved returns will receive a full refund of the product price. Original shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to Auraaya error. Refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 7 business days of receiving the returned item.

6. Can I exchange for a different item or size?

Yes. Exchanges are subject to stock availability. If the new item is of higher value, the price difference must be paid. If of lower value, the difference will be refunded. Exchange requests must be made within the 14-day return period.

7. What condition must the item be in?

All returned items must be unworn, undamaged, and returned with the original Auraaya gift box, anti-tarnish lining, polishing cloth, and certificate of authenticity. Items showing signs of wear will not be accepted.

8. What if my order arrived damaged or defective?

Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photographs of the damage. Auraaya will arrange for a free replacement or repair, including collection and return shipping at no cost to you.

Response Time

Customer Service & Response Times

1. What are your customer service hours?

Auraaya customer support is available Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 SAST, excluding South African public holidays.

2. How long does it take to receive a response?

All enquiries submitted via email to auraaya@gmail.com or through the Contact Us form will receive a response within 24 business hours. Enquiries received after 17:00 or on weekends will be addressed on the next business day.

3. Do you offer phone support?

At present, Auraaya provides support via email and the website contact form only. This allows us to maintain accurate records of all correspondence and provide detailed assistance regarding your jewellery order.

4. What is the response time for order status updates?

Order confirmation is sent immediately upon successful payment. Dispatch notifications with tracking details are sent within 1 business day of your order being shipped. For any order-related queries, we will respond within 24 business hours.

5. How quickly are returns and refunds processed?

Return authorisation requests are reviewed within 24 business hours. Once Auraaya receives and inspects the returned item, refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7 business days.
